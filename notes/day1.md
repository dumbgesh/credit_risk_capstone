##Today's focus

What is credit?
> Credit means borrowing money or something of value now and paying for it later.Credit Risk is the risk that the lender faces while lending you the money, because they need to estimate whether you're likely to repay it later.
> Lender lends money only if the borrower is creditworthy. Now how to decide who is creditworthy?

What is lending?
> Lending is the process of giving the credit to the borrower with the expectation that it'll be repaid, usually with intrest.
> Lender lends money with the expectation that they'll get their money back (the principal) with intrest. 

What is a loan?
> Loan is a specific amount of money borrowed by a person/ organization that must be repaid according to agreed terms.
> A loan normally specifies: the principal amount, the intrest, repayment period, repayment schedule, fees/charge and consequences of non-payment

What is default?
> Default occurs when a borrower is fails to repay the loan. 
> Late payment is not loan, it is delinquency. Defualt is a more serious failure, which risks the expected cash flow of a lender.
> Lender cares about default because lending puts the money of the lender at risk, and deafult leads the lender to losses.

What is Probability of Default (PD)?
> Probability of Default is the estimated probability that a borrower will default. Ranges from 0 to 1.
> A lender uses PD to make decisions such as, approving/ rejecting borrowers, setting credit limits, determine pricing/ intrest rates, and estimate loses

>For each concept, understand what it means and why a lender cares about it.
